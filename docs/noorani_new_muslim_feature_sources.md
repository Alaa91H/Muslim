# Qaida Noorania and New Muslim Corner: Content and Media Notes

## Scope and pedagogical approach

The feature is a **beginner orientation**, not a reproduction of the Qaida Noorania or Baghdadia books. It presents the Arabic alphabet, simplified visual memory cues for broad articulation areas, short-vowel / lengthening / sukūn practice, and a clear instruction to learn pronunciation from a qualified Arabic or Quran teacher. The visual cue is deliberately described as a cue rather than a technical diagram of every articulation point.

The New Muslim Corner is written as supportive education. It explicitly affirms that accepting Islam is a free personal choice, offers a short statement of faith for people who already believe it, introduces wudu at a high level, and encourages paced learning, privacy, and trusted local support. It does not issue an individual fatwa and notes that practical details can differ among recognised scholarly schools.

## Audio and visual licensing decision

No third-party Qaida recordings, book scans, diagrams, logos, or video embeds are bundled or copied. Search results identified lessons from commercial/private websites and social-video platforms, but no sufficiently clear redistributable licence was verified for packaging in the application.

Instead, the screen uses:

1. **On-device Android text-to-speech** for isolated Arabic letter and short practice prompts, contingent on an Arabic voice installed by the user.
2. **Original Compose-drawn visual cues** that mark a broad memory area, not copied anatomical illustrations or textbook pages.
3. Clear, in-product language that device speech and visual cues do not replace qualified teacher feedback.

This keeps the initial section interactive while avoiding unverified copyright claims or distribution of educational media without permission.

## Release-review requirement

As with the project religious-content policy, the pronunciation cues, translations, beginner wording, and practical purification sequence require specialist Arabic/Quran and Islamic-studies review before an official religious-content release.
